## Where Freshdesk wins on paper

Freshdesk's free tier is a genuinely easy on-ramp, and the Freshworks suite (CRM, marketing)
integrates tightly if you're already using those products. Its UI has had years of SMB-focused
polish.

## Where it doesn't fit

Both free tiers cap out by agent count — five on Salamandr Community. The difference is what
the cap costs you underneath it. Freshdesk's free plan holds back SLA policies, automation and
most channels for paid tiers, so a small team on it is also a *feature-limited* team.
Salamandr's five agents get every channel, real SLA calendars, bot flows and the plugin system
with no separate paywall inside Community itself — the one thing Enterprise adds beyond the
seat cap is a short list of ready-made integrations this project builds and maintains
(WooCommerce/Shopify, Slack/Teams, HubSpot-style CRM connectors — see
[Editions](../../docs/editions/)), not a second tier of the core product.
