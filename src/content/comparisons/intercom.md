## Where Intercom wins on paper

If your support flow genuinely starts with a chat bubble inside your own web or mobile app,
Intercom's messaging-first design and product-usage context are purpose-built for that, in a
way a ticket-first tool has to work harder to match.

## Where it doesn't fit

Two things trip teams up: pricing that mixes seats with usage-based components is hard to
forecast against a growing conversation volume, and — same as every entry on this page — the
data stays on Intercom's cloud. If what you need is a ticket-first helpdesk across email, chat
and messaging apps with a predictable flat cost (or none at all, self-hosted), that's a
different product shape than Intercom is optimized for.
