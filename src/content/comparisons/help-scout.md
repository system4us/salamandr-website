## Where Help Scout wins on paper

If a team's real problem is that their ticketing tool feels bureaucratic — case numbers,
rigid queues, a UI that reminds customers they're "a ticket" — Help Scout's stripped-down,
email-like inbox solves exactly that, and does it well.

## Where it doesn't fit

Simplicity is a ceiling as much as a feature. Real SLA calendars, multi-channel bot flows, and
extensibility via a WASM plugin SDK — all free in Community — aren't things Help Scout is
trying to do — its whole positioning is to not be that tool. Salamandr covers the same "doesn't
feel bureaucratic" ground through its customer self-service portal and simple channel adapters,
without giving up the operational depth a growing support team eventually needs.
