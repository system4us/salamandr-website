## Where osTicket wins on paper

osTicket is completely free, open source, and has been around for over a decade. For teams looking for a traditional email-inbox ticket manager that runs on standard shared web hosting with a LAMP stack (Linux, Apache, MySQL, PHP), osTicket is well-understood and lightweight to operate.

## Where it doesn't fit

Modern customer expectations have shifted far beyond slow email threads. Today's users expect live chat, WhatsApp, and social messaging directly in the same workflow. 

While osTicket handles basic email queues, modern operations quickly hit limitations: there are no native interactive chat widgets, no visual triage bots, no real-time team chat mirroring (Discord/Slack/Teams), and no integrated calendar bookings or e-commerce context. 

Salamandr delivers the best of both worlds: the full privacy, self-hosting, and data ownership you expect from open source, combined with the real-time omnichannel speed and automation of a modern customer support platform.
