## Where Zendesk wins on paper

Zendesk's app marketplace and integration breadth are the product of a decade of ecosystem
investment — if you need an off-the-shelf integration for something niche, it more often
already exists there. Its AI features and workflow automation are also more mature simply by
virtue of scale and R&D budget.

## Where it doesn't fit

None of that changes who holds your data. For a healthcare provider, a public-sector team, or
anyone under EU data-residency rules, "cloud-only" isn't a feature gap you can integrate your
way around — it's a structural fact about where the database lives. Salamandr's answer isn't
to out-feature Zendesk; it's to make self-hosting a first-class, fully-functional option
instead of an afterthought, with the same channel coverage and automation a modern support
team actually uses day to day.
