## Eliminating the Tab-Switching Bottleneck in E-Commerce Support

In modern e-commerce operations, support volume spikes during seasonal promotions, product drops, and holiday peaks. A high percentage of customer inquiries are repetitive questions regarding shipping updates, sizing guidance, return labels, and order modifications.

When agents must switch between WhatsApp, Instagram, email inboxes, and separate storefront dashboards, average handling time doubles.

## Native Omnichannel with Turnkey Storefront Context

Salamandr brings customer messaging and live store data together into a single, cohesive workspace:
- **Turnkey Shopify & WooCommerce Integration:** As soon as an agent opens a ticket, Salamandr automatically matches the customer's email or phone number against your store. The sidebar displays order history, items purchased, tracking links, and fulfillment status in real time.
- **On-Demand Actions:** Issue refunds, apply store credit coupons, or generate return labels directly from the ticket without opening the e-commerce back office.
- **Custom ERP and Warehouse Systems:** If you operate a custom inventory management system (WMS), ERP, or logistics API, a lightweight WebAssembly extension displays tracking numbers and warehouse dispatch stages right in the ticket view.

## Visual Automation Bots and Business Hours SLAs

- **Triage Decision-Tree Bots:** Set up interactive self-service bots on WhatsApp, Instagram, and Live Chat that let customers check their delivery status or browse return policies 24/7 without agent involvement.
- **Operational SLA Calendars:** Create custom business hours schedules reflecting warehouse dispatch shifts and customer service hours, ensuring response deadlines pause when the support desk is offline.
